\section{Conclusion}
On a domain-disjoint EBBU/HP benchmark with a frozen, source-only learning protocol, lexical URL classifiers transferred well from EBBU to HP (F1 \res{F1ABLGBMALL} for LightGBM) but lost \res{DropBLGBMALL}--\res{DropBRFALL} F1 points from HP to EBBU (RQ1). Stability ranking gave higher target F1 than mean ranking at the same budget in the harder direction (\res{DeltaBARF} RF, \res{DeltaBALGBM} LightGBM), without a clear advantage in the other direction; reduced subsets did not match the full feature set across datasets (RQ2). Latency trade-offs depend on the model and execution setting (RQ3). Exploratory corrected SHAP comparisons pass strict numerical additivity while retaining descriptive, non-causal scope (RQ4). The contribution is a reproducible empirical comparison under a defined target-access constraint, not a new classifier or a demonstrated improvement over UDA. Future work should assess explanation fidelity beyond additivity, evaluate more recent independent sources, and quantify training variability with repeated splits under a new protocol before opening a new holdout.
